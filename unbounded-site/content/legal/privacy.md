## 1. Who we are

Unbounded Spac3s is a trading name of bigBOXLILbox Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 12522653). Our registered office is 367 Caledonian Road, London N7 9DQ. We decide how your personal information is used, which makes us the "controller" under UK data protection law. Our ICO registration number is ZC289394.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. If you have a question, email [hello@unboundedspac3s.com](mailto:hello@unboundedspac3s.com). We're a small business, so a real person will reply.

## 2. What we collect and where it comes from

**Information you give us**

- **Find Your Fit quiz and other checks:** your answers, your email address, your name and business name if you share them, and whether you've agreed to hear from us.
- **The question box on our home page:** whatever you type in.
- **Enquiries:** your name, email address and message.
- **Purchases:** your name, email address, billing details and what you bought. Payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see your full card number.

**Information collected automatically**

Like most websites, our hosting provider records technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This keeps the site secure and working. We also use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It doesn't use cookies and only keeps anonymised figures, so it can't be used to identify you or follow you around the internet.

**Information from public sources**

If you're a director of a recently formed limited company in a sector we work with, we may collect your name, company name and business email address. We get these from the public Companies House register and from your company's own website or public business listings.

Please don't put health details or other sensitive information into our quiz or question box. They don't need it. Our site isn't aimed at anyone under 18.

## 3. How we use it and why we're allowed to

The law requires a lawful basis for each use of your information. Here are ours.

| What we do | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- |
| Give you your quiz results and recommendations | Contract: providing the service you asked for |
| Process orders and deliver your downloads | Contract |
| Keep tax and accounting records | Legal obligation |
| Send marketing emails to people who signed up | Consent |
| Contact directors of new limited companies about our services | Legitimate interests |
| Answer enquiries, requests and complaints | Legitimate interests |
| Keep the site secure, fix problems and understand how it's used | Legitimate interests |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we've checked that our interest in running and growing the business doesn't outweigh your rights. For outreach, that means business contact details only, a handful of personal emails a week, and an easy way to say no.

## 4. Marketing emails and how to stop them

If you take our quiz, we'll only send you marketing emails if you tick the box to say yes. The box is never ticked for you, and you'll get your results either way.

If you run a limited company or LLP, we may send you an occasional personal email about services that fit your sector. UK law allows this for incorporated businesses without asking first. Every email says who we are and how to stop hearing from us. We don't send these to sole traders or personal email addresses unless they've agreed.

You can opt out at any time by clicking unsubscribe, replying "stop" or emailing [hello@unboundedspac3s.com](mailto:hello@unboundedspac3s.com). We'll keep your email address on a do-not-contact list so we don't contact you again by mistake.

We never sell or rent your information to anyone.

## 5. AI tools on our site

The question box on our home page uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. When you use it, what you type is sent to Anthropic to produce your answer. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models, and we don't keep a copy.

Our Find Your Fit quiz works on fixed rules we wrote, not AI. No decision with a legal or similarly serious effect on you is made automatically, and a person reads any enquiry before we follow up.

AI can get things wrong. Treat the answers as a helpful starting point, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Some of our providers store information in the USA. Where they do, it's protected by the UK-US data bridge for certified companies, or by the UK's approved contract clauses for international transfers. Email us if you'd like more detail.

| Information | How long |

| Marketing list | Until you unsubscribe |

## 9. Cookies

Our site only uses cookies it needs to work. Our visitor statistics come from Vercel Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies at all, so there's nothing for you to accept or turn off. When you click through to buy, Payhip and Stripe may set their own cookies to complete your purchase. If we ever add tracking that uses cookies, we'll update this section and ask for your permission where the law requires it.

## 10. Your rights and how to complain

You have the right to:

- see the information we hold about you
- have it corrected if it's wrong
- have it deleted
- limit how we use it
- object to how we use it, including a complete right to say no to marketing
- receive a copy in a format you can take elsewhere
- withdraw your consent at any time

To use any of these, email [hello@unboundedspac3s.com](mailto:hello@unboundedspac3s.com) with "Privacy" in the subject line. It's free, and we'll reply within one month.

**Complaints**

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. Email [hello@unboundedspac3s.com](mailto:hello@unboundedspac3s.com) with "Privacy complaint" in the subject line. We'll acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it properly and tell you the outcome without delay.

If you're still not satisfied, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint) or on 0303 123 1113.

We'll update this page when the way we use information changes, and show the new date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let people on our mailing list know.
